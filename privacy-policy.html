Privacy Policy

Acorn Rumble: Beetle Bandits (Android) · Old Oak Games · Effective 5 October 2026

In short: the game doesn't ask for your name, email or any account, has no chat, and keeps your progress only on your device. It shows ads from Google AdMob that are set up for children: every ad request is child-directed, limited to G-rated ads and never personalised, and the game doesn't access your device's advertising ID. Old Oak Games itself collects no personal information.

1. Who we are

Acorn Rumble: Beetle Bandits ("the game") is made by Old Oak Games ("we", "us"). You can reach us at acornrumble@gmail.com.

2. Information stored on your device

The game saves your stars, best scores and sound setting in the app's own storage on your device so you can pick up where you left off. This information never leaves your device and is never sent to us. Uninstalling the game, or clearing its storage in your device's settings, deletes it.

3. Advertising

The game is free and is paid for by ads from Google AdMob, using the Google Mobile Ads SDK:

  • Rewarded video ads are optional. One plays only if the player taps "Watch an ad for 1 more acorn".
  • Interstitial (full-screen) ads appear now and then between levels: never when the game starts, never before level 3, and no more than about once every two minutes.

Because children may play, the game tells Google that every ad request is child-directed and asks for G-rated ads only. Google then serves only non-personalised ads, without interest-based advertising or remarketing. The game's manifest also removes the permission to read the device's advertising ID.

To deliver ads, measure them and prevent fraud, Google's SDK may collect limited information from the device:

  • IP address, used to estimate general location (such as country or city)
  • Device and app information, such as device model, operating system version, language, app version and an app-set identifier
  • Ad interactions, such as ad views and taps, and app launches
  • Diagnostic information, such as crash and performance data

This information is sent to Google encrypted (TLS). Google processes it under its own policies: the Google Privacy Policy and How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services.

In the European Economic Area, the UK and Switzerland, Google's consent tools may show a consent message before ads load. The game marks these requests as coming from users under the age of consent.

4. What Old Oak Games collects

Nothing. We don't run servers, analytics or crash reporting of our own, and we never sell or rent personal information. If you email us, we use your email address and message only to reply, and delete the conversation when it is no longer needed.

5. Children

The game is made for everyone, including children. We don't knowingly collect personal information from anyone, children included. The game has no accounts, chat, social features, in-app purchases or links that take players out of the game. Ads are configured as described in section 3. Parents with questions can write to acornrumble@gmail.com.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Delete your saved progress at any time by clearing the game's storage or uninstalling it.
  • Depending on where you live, you may have rights to access or delete personal information, or to object to its use. We hold no personal information about players. For information processed by Google, see Google's privacy policy; we are glad to help you with any request.

7. Security

Data sent by the ads SDK is encrypted in transit. Because we don't collect personal information, there is none for us to store or lose.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the effective date above.
